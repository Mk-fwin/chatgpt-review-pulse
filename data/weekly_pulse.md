# Weekly Product Pulse: Groww (Android)
Reviews: 7 Aug to 3 Oct 2026, 9,000 Play Store reviews (1,596 with 8+ words)

## Top themes (ranked by low-rated mentions)
1. **App stability and trading UX** (397 mentions)
2. **Customer support** (227 mentions, average 1.67 stars)
3. **Charges and pricing clarity** (199 mentions)

Strength: ease of use (360 mentions, average 4.22 stars).

## User quotes
- "my trade for only 1 mint and 1 order after that It will deduct 200+ ₹ charges why? but you mentioned that 20₹/ per order" (1 star, 14 Aug)
- "Worst customer care service, no reply for last few of my complaints till now" (1 star, 21 Sep)
- "When we open the charts the back button on our phone just disappears" (1 star, 28 Aug)

## Key observation
Users compare the advertised ₹20 brokerage with what is actually deducted. 195 long reviews mention charges, 119 of them rated 1 or 2 stars: 29 call charges hidden or unclear, 22 say brokerage is higher than advertised, and 20 say charges eat their profit. Exit load appears in none, so mutual fund fees are not the issue. Slow support replies add to the frustration.

## Action ideas
1. Show an itemised charge preview (brokerage, DP, statutory, GST) before an order is confirmed.
2. Add a "why was I charged" breakdown to trade confirmations, linking to the brokerage calculator.
3. Give support one standard reply for charge queries, and track repeat contacts about charges.
