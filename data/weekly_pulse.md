# Weekly Product Pulse: ChatGPT India (Android)
Reviews: 9 Aug to 3 Oct 2026, 14,975 Play Store reviews (1 to 3 stars only)

## Top themes (share of long reviews)
1. **Usage limits and plan value** (21%)
2. **Answer quality and instruction-following** (15.8%)
3. **App bugs, crashes and slowness** (14.7%)

## User quotes
- "I managed to burn through my entire Plus subscriptions allowance in 4 hours of work." (1 star, 24 Sep)
- "It has stopped following 95% of the directions given to it." (1 star, 11 Sep)
- "The app gets stuck in an infinite "thinking" loop constantly." (1 star, 29 Aug)

## Key observation
Users cannot tell what free and paid plans include. 228 reviews say they paid and still hit limits; 132 say it is unclear what they are paying for. OpenAI's own pages also disagree: the Go FAQ says voice has Free-tier limits, while the Voice page lists 3 hours per day for Go. Voice appears in 328 reviews (2.2%, average 1.84 stars), mostly about limits ending after 5 to 10 minutes and background noise interrupting.

## Action ideas
1. Show a usage meter and reset time in-app, including voice minutes left.
2. Align the pricing page, help articles and upgrade screen into one India-specific limits table.
3. Add an interruption-sensitivity toggle and an automatic switch to dictation when the voice limit is hit.
