# Groww charges: support explainer
- The advertised ₹20 is brokerage only: the lower of ₹20 or 0.1% of trade value per order, with a minimum charge of ₹5 (or 2.5% of trade value, if that is lower).
- Other charges are added on top: statutory and exchange charges (STT, stamp duty, exchange transaction charges, SEBI turnover charge, investor protection fund charge). These are levied by the exchange and government, not set by Groww.
- 18% GST applies on brokerage, DP charges and exchange-related charges.
- Selling delivery shares adds a DP charge per sell transaction: ₹16.50 (Groww) plus a depository fee of ₹3.50 (₹3.25 for women), plus GST. Groww's part is waived when the sell value is under ₹100.
- Intraday positions not squared off before 3:20 PM are charged ₹50 plus GST per position.
- Because these are separate line items, total charges on one trade can exceed ₹20. The Brokerage Calculator and the charges report itemise each one.
