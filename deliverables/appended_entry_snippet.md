## User quotes

## Key observation
Users cannot tell what free and paid plans include. 228 reviews say they paid and still hit limits; 132 say it is unclear what they are paying for. OpenAI's own pages also disagree: the Go FAQ says voice has Free-tier limits, while the Voice page lists 3 hours per day for Go. Voice appears in 328 reviews (2.2%, average 1.84 stars), mostly about limits ending after 5 to 10 minutes and background noise interrupting.

## Action ideas
1. Show a usage meter and reset time in-app, including voice minutes left.
2. Align the pricing page, help articles and upgrade screen into one India-specific limits table.
3. Add an interruption-sensitivity toggle and an automatic switch to dictation when the voice limit is hit.


**Explanation bullets:**
- Everyday text chats are unlimited on Free, Go, Plus and Pro (subject to abuse guardrails); limits apply to features such as image generation, file uploads, data analysis and voice.
- Voice in Chat is measured over a rolling 24 hours, and ChatGPT notifies you at the limit: Free has limited access; Go and Plus each get 3 hours; Pro gets 15 hours or unlimited, depending on tier.
- A Voice conversation can end when you reach a usage limit; you can continue in text or start Voice again.
- Go is billed automatically each month and can be canceled anytime; annual or multi-month prepayment is not offered. Prices are on the pricing page.
- If you subscribed through the Play Store or App Store, the upgrade may take a few minutes to appear; wait and restart the app.
- Switching from Plus or Pro to Go gives no refund; your current plan runs to the end of the billing cycle. Incorrect charges can be raised through the refund request process.
